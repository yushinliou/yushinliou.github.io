## The Challenge

Weddings are deeply meaningful rituals that bring families together across generations. How can we add meaning to a ritual already so well-established and layered with many smaller rituals?

## The opportunity

We conducted **7 semi-structured interviews** with couples married within the last 5 years, complemented by a literature review on wedding rituals and technology's role in memory-making.

## Why Timeline?

Minimizing disruption during moment capture requires more automation, but this creates overwhelming content that obscures what truly matters. Any additional device inevitably intrudes on the experience.

<div class="max-w-3xl mx-auto bg-gradient-to-br from-rose-50 to-amber-50 dark:from-gray-800 dark:to-gray-800 rounded-2xl p-10 border border-rose-100 dark:border-gray-700">
Yet the most genuine, moving moments are often already captured in guests' phones; they're just never collected and brought together.
</div>

We selected the **collaborative timeline** because it uniquely resolves the core tension:

<div class="my-8 space-y-6">
  <div class="flex gap-6 items-center">
    <i class="fa-solid fa-clock text-3xl w-10 text-center"></i>
    <div>
      <p class="font-medium m-0 leading-tight">No disruption during the wedding</p>
      <p class="text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400 m-0 mt-1">Content is uploaded afterward, at guests' convenience</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-6 items-center">
    <i class="fa-solid fa-users text-3xl w-10 text-center"></i>
    <div>
      <p class="font-medium m-0 leading-tight">Co-creation adds depth</p>
      <p class="text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400 m-0 mt-1">Multiple perspectives transform isolated moments into rich narratives</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-6 items-center">
    <i class="fa-solid fa-camera text-3xl w-10 text-center"></i>
    <div>
      <p class="font-medium m-0 leading-tight">Encourages intentional capturing</p>
      <p class="text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400 m-0 mt-1">Knowing there's a place to share motivates mindful photography</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## Key Findings & Iterations

<div class="space-y-4 my-8">
  <details class="group bg-white dark:bg-gray-800 rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-medium flex justify-between items-center">
      <span>1. Confusing dual "Add" buttons</span>
      <span class="text-rose-500 group-open:-rotate-90 transition-transform">◀</span>
    </summary>
    <div class="px-4 pb-4 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400">
      <p><strong>Finding:</strong> Users couldn't distinguish between the Add button on Timeline vs. Moment View.</p>
      <p class="m-0"><strong>Solution:</strong> Redesigned to show two clear options: "Add Media" (to existing moment) or "Add Moment" (create new).</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="group bg-white dark:bg-gray-800 rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-medium flex justify-between items-center">
      <span>2. Desire to see contributor identity</span>
      <span class="text-rose-500 group-open:-rotate-90 transition-transform">◀</span>
    </summary>
    <div class="px-4 pb-4 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400">
      <p><strong>Finding:</strong> Couples wanted to know "What did Mom/friends capture?"</p>
      <p class="m-0"><strong>Solution:</strong> Added contributor identity display on moments and individual media.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="group bg-white dark:bg-gray-800 rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-medium flex justify-between items-center">
      <span>3. Need for flexible input methods</span>
      <span class="text-rose-500 group-open:-rotate-90 transition-transform">◀</span>
    </summary>
    <div class="px-4 pb-4 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400">
      <p><strong>Finding:</strong> Users wanted text descriptions in addition to voice memos.</p>
      <p class="m-0"><strong>Solution:</strong> Added parallel options: Photo, Video, and Voice Memo.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="group bg-white dark:bg-gray-800 rounded-lg border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-medium flex justify-between items-center">
      <span>4. Couple's view should feel special</span>
      <span class="text-rose-500 group-open:-rotate-90 transition-transform">◀</span>
    </summary>
    <div class="px-4 pb-4 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400">
      <p><strong>Finding:</strong> Couples wanted their wedding page to feel distinct from weddings they attended as guests.</p>
      <p class="m-0"><strong>Solution:</strong> Created visual differentiation with welcome message and prominent hero image for the couple's own wedding.</p>

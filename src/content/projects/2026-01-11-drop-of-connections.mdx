## The Ocean Within

Notice that those dots move as if they are lightly stepped on water.

This motion comes from a metaphor about life:

> *When you're born,*
> *you are like a single drop of water,*
> *flying upward,*
> *separated from the one giant consciousness.*
>
> *You get older. You descend back down.*
>
> *You die.*
>
> *You land back into the water, become one with the ocean again.*


Each drop on the screen represents a fragment of my life, a trace of my consciousness.

They seem randomly drifting, scattered across a broad universe, but their movement is never entirely their own. Their motion is subtly shaped by the force beneath the surface: **the ocean of collective consciousness.**


## And then there is you.

You are here.

You *disturb* the flow. You *interrupt*, *unsettle*, and *rearrange* those drops, and allows new connections to form.

**How would you change my life, stranger?**
